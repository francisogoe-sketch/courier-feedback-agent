📖  Glossary & Calculation Rules

▼ click to expand
Q1 Rating (1-5)
1=Very Dissatisfied, 2=Dissatisfied, 3=Neutral, 4=Satisfied, 5=Very Satisfied
csat_positive = 1 if Q1 in {3,4,5} csat_negative = 1 if Q1 in {1,2}
Positive CSAT %
% of valid Q1 responses rated 3, 4 or 5
SUM(csat_positive) / SUM(csat_valid) x 100
🟢 >=60% On target🟡 50-60% Watch🔴 less 50% Action
Negative CSAT %
% of valid Q1 responses rated 1 or 2
SUM(csat_negative) / SUM(csat_valid) x 100
🟢 less 18%🟡 18-22%🔴 more 22% Action
X - Active Denominator
Total of Pillars 1-4. Pillar 5 NEVER included in X.
X = P1(1,303) + P2(280) + P3(1,375) + P4(707) = 3,665
Pillar Metrics [1]-[4]
[1] Internal neg vs pos within this pillar
[2] Pillar share of all Q1 negative ratings
[3] Pillar negative as % of all valid Q1
[4] Per-market contribution to this pillar negative
[1] neg_in_pillar / total_in_pillar x 100 [2] neg_in_pillar / total_Q1_negative x 100 [3] neg_in_pillar / total_Q1_valid x 100 [4] market_neg_in_pillar / total_neg_in_pillar x 100
Q2 Resolution Rate
% answering Yes to "Did we resolve your issue?" -- IL responses in Hebrew (yes/no auto-detected)
SUM(resolved) / COUNT(*) x 100
🟢 >=70%🟡 50-69%🔴 less 50%
