▼ Dashboard Glossary
Q1 (Rating)
Courier satisfaction rating. 1–2★ = negative, 3–5★ = positive.
Q2 (Resolution)
Whether the courier's issue was resolved during the support interaction. "Yes" = resolved, "No" = not resolved.
Q3 (Free Text)
Courier's open-ended feedback comment submitted with the survey.
Negative Q1 %
Share of transcripts where the courier rated 1–2★.
Positive Q1 %
Share of transcripts where the courier rated 3–5★.
Resolution Rate
Share of transcripts where the issue was resolved (Q2 = "Yes").
Market Contribution to Negative Q1
Each market's share of total negative Q1 transcripts in the selected period.
Market Contribution to Positive Q1
Each market's share of total positive Q1 transcripts in the selected period. Shows which markets have the most satisfied couriers.
Pillar Categories
Feedback is classified into 5 operational pillars: Support Quality, App / Tech Issues, Partner and External Delays, Compensation, and General / Minimal.
Gold Couriers
Couriers in the Gold tier of the courier loyalty/rewards program (UK and CA markets).
Courier Filter
Type or select a courier ID to filter all dashboard metrics and transcripts to that individual courier only. Clear the field to show all couriers.
Custom Range
Select a start and end date to view transcripts within an arbitrary date range.
Q1 Without Q3
Couriers who submitted a Q1 satisfaction rating but did not leave a Q3 free-text comment. Shown with their rating (negative = 1-2 stars, positive = 3-5 stars). When Gold Only is checked, only gold-tier couriers appear in this list.
vs All-time
Change in percentage points (pp) between the selected period value and the all-time average. ▲ = increase, ▼ = decrease. Green = good change, red = bad change.
